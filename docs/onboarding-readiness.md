# BIMED onboarding readiness gate

Staff creation requires a signed employment contract and all required onboarding checklist items to be completed or explicitly waived by an administrator.

## Required checks

Local candidates require identity verification, qualification evidence verification, reference verification, and right-to-work verification.

Candidates living outside Ireland additionally require international work-permission verification.

## Informational packets

Candidate-facing guides such as Welcome to Ireland and Care in Ireland are not treated as completion gates. Issuing or viewing an informational packet does not by itself mark an onboarding requirement complete.

## Waivers

A required checklist item may be waived only by an administrator and only with a note explaining the reason. Every completion, waiver, and reset is recorded in the recruitment audit log.
