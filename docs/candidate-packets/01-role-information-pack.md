# BIMED Healthcare Role Information Pack

This guide explains the role you have been shortlisted for, the working expectations, and the checks that must be completed before employment starts.

## 1. About BIMED Healthcare

BIMED Healthcare provides care and support services with a focus on safe, respectful, person-centred practice. Every worker is expected to protect dignity, privacy, wellbeing, and choice and to work within their competence and assigned responsibilities.

## 2. Your role

Your candidate-specific role details are shown by BIMED from your application and formal employment documents. Your final contract is the controlling source for your agreed title, location, hours, pay, leave, notice, and other employment terms.

## 3. Main purpose of the role

The role is to provide safe, compassionate, reliable support to service users in accordance with their care plan, BIMED policies, training, and applicable Irish requirements.

## 4. Core duties

- Support service users with agreed daily-living activities and independence.
- Observe and report changes in health, wellbeing, behaviour, or presentation.
- Follow care plans, risk assessments, safeguarding procedures, infection-prevention measures, and escalation routes.
- Maintain accurate, timely, and confidential records.
- Communicate respectfully with service users, families, colleagues, and relevant professionals.
- Attend required induction, supervision, training, and competency assessments.
- Work within professional and organisational boundaries and escalate concerns promptly.

## 5. Person specification

**Essential:**
- Genuine commitment to person-centred care.
- Appropriate experience or qualification for the advertised role.
- Ability to communicate clearly and work reliably as part of a team.
- Ability to follow instructions, record information accurately, and escalate concerns.
- Satisfactory identity, employment, reference, safeguarding, and eligibility checks required for the role.

**Desirable:**
- Relevant healthcare or social-care qualification.
- Previous experience in Irish or equivalent regulated care environments.
- Additional care, safeguarding, manual handling, first-aid, infection-prevention, or medication-related training where relevant to the role.

## 6. Working expectations

Your employment contract and roster determine your agreed hours, location, pay, leave, notice, and other contractual terms. This guide is an orientation document and does not replace those formal terms.

## 7. Recruitment and clearance

Appointment remains subject to the checks and conditions communicated by BIMED, which may include identity, references, qualification or credential verification, right-to-work or employment-permit checks, Garda vetting where applicable, occupational or role-specific requirements, and completion of onboarding documentation.

## 8. International candidates

Candidates living outside Ireland may receive additional immigration, relocation, and arrival information. Any sponsorship or employment-permit support remains subject to BIMED's eligibility assessment and the applicable Irish process. BIMED does not provide personal legal or immigration advice through this guide.
