# BIMED International Recruitment & Work-Permission Information

This section is for candidates being considered from outside Ireland. Complete the secure form shown on this page. BIMED will receive your responses with your candidate record.

This information is used for recruitment and, where appropriate, preparation for the relevant employment-permit and immigration process. It is administrative guidance, not immigration or legal advice.

## Important

Receiving this form or an employment offer does not itself create a right to work in Ireland or guarantee an employment permit, visa, or entry permission. The relevant authorities make immigration and employment-permit decisions.

## What you will provide

You will confirm your current residence and work-permission position, provide passport details, give an expected relocation date, and answer a small number of previous-permission questions.

## Evidence you may later be asked to provide

Depending on the role and process, BIMED may request:

- Passport identity page.
- Current immigration or work-permission evidence.
- Qualification certificates.
- Professional registration or licence evidence where relevant.
- CV and employment history.
- Employment references.
- Evidence required for the applicable employment-permit route.
- Other role-specific evidence requested by BIMED.

Use only the secure submission route provided by BIMED. Never email passwords, PINs, banking security codes, or one-time authentication codes.

## Before you submit

Check that your passport details are accurate and that your answers reflect your current circumstances. Where a question is unclear, contact BIMED recruitment rather than guessing.
