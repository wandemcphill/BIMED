# BIMED International Recruitment & Visa Sponsorship Pack

**Document status:** Controlled template  
**Audience:** Candidates recruited from outside Ireland  
**Important:** This pack is administrative guidance, not immigration or legal advice.

## 1. Purpose

This pack collects the information BIMED needs to assess an overseas candidate's proposed recruitment and, where appropriate, prepare information required for the relevant employment-permit and immigration process.

Being invited to complete this pack, or receiving an employment offer, does not itself create a right to work in Ireland or guarantee an employment permit, visa, or entry permission.
