# BIMED Supporting Documents Checklist

Use the interactive checklist on this page to keep track of the evidence BIMED has asked you to prepare. BIMED will tell you when a document needs to be submitted and how to submit it securely.

## Identity

- Passport or approved identity evidence.
- Current address evidence where requested.
- Relevant immigration permission evidence, where applicable.

## Employment and experience

- Current CV.
- Previous employment details.
- Reference details.
- Employment/reference evidence requested by BIMED.

## Qualifications and registration

- Qualification certificates.
- Professional registration/licence, where the role requires it.
- Training certificates relevant to the role.
- Credential verification information requested by BIMED.

## International employment

- Work-permission information.
- Employment-permit information or evidence requested for the applicable process.
- Relocation information.
- Candidate information requested for the international recruitment process.

## Role-specific evidence

- Additional safeguarding or vetting information where applicable.
- Occupational or fitness documentation where law or policy requires it.
- Any other evidence specifically requested for the role.

## Submission

Ticking an item confirms that you have it ready. It does not mean BIMED has received, verified, or approved the document.

Use only the secure submission route provided by BIMED. Never send passwords, PINs, authentication codes, or banking security credentials.
