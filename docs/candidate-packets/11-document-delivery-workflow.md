# BIMED Document Delivery Workflow

The candidate document system is controlled by recruitment stage and pathway.

## Shortlist
Role Information Pack, Candidate Application Guide, Interview Questionnaire, and Supporting Documents Checklist are the standard candidate-facing materials.

## Employment offer / onboarding
The admin onboarding action issues the **BIMED employment offer package**, including the employment contract, role-specific job description, and employee handbook. The offer action records an auditable event and sends the candidate a single onboarding email.

The employment contract is the formal contractual document used for signature. Candidates should retain a copy after signing; the Staff Portal provides a signed-contract copy after activation.

## International pathway
Candidates who answered that they do not live in Ireland receive the International Recruitment & Visa Sponsorship Pack, Welcome to Ireland Pack, and International Relocation & First-Month Checklist in addition to the standard onboarding materials.

## New starter
The New Starter Onboarding Checklist stays with the employee through pre-first-shift checks, Day 1, Week 1 and first-month review.

## Permanent employee identity
When a candidate reaches Selected, Offer Issued, or Onboarding status, the application can be linked to a permanent BIMED staff record. Subsequent rota, attendance, payroll and compliance processes use that staff identity.

## Security and governance
Document source files are controlled templates. Candidate links must be generated server-side and expire. Sensitive credentials, banking security information and immigration outcomes must never be represented as guaranteed by BIMED. Legal/HR approval remains required before contractual templates are used in production.
