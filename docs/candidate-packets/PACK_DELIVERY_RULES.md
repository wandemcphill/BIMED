# BIMED Candidate Pack Delivery Rules

## Stage 1: Shortlist invitation
Attach links to the role information pack and candidate application guide. Tell the candidate which supporting evidence BIMED may require.

## Stage 2: Interview
Provide the controlled interview questionnaire and collect responses through the portal's written/audio workflow.

## Stage 3: Selection / offer preparation
Admin reviews references, eligibility, credentials and international-pathway requirements. No employment contract is issued until BIMED has approved the applicable terms.

## Stage 4: Offer issued
The portal can issue the complete pack in one action. Ireland-based candidates receive the standard employment/onboarding documents. Candidates living outside Ireland additionally receive the international sponsorship, Welcome to Ireland and relocation documents.

## Stage 5: Pre-arrival and onboarding
International candidates work through pre-travel and arrival checklists. All new starters receive the onboarding checklist and Care in Ireland handbook before starting relevant duties.

## Stage 6: Permanent staff lifecycle
Once the candidate reaches the selected/offer/onboarding pathway, the application is linked to a permanent BIMED staff identity. Rota, attendance, payroll and future compliance records use that staff identity.

## Safety controls
Candidate-facing links are generated server-side, expire, and are tied to the application. No secret token or raw storage path is placed in public document content. Immigration, tax, employment and regulatory statements remain controlled HR guidance and are not presented as personal legal advice.
