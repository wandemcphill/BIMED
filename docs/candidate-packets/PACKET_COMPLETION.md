# BIMED Candidate Packet Completion Standard

A candidate packet is complete only when required stage documents, pathway documents, signatures and audit records are present.

## Shortlist
Role Information Pack, Candidate Application Guide, Interview Questionnaire and Supporting Documents Checklist are the standard candidate-facing materials.

## Offer / onboarding
Employment Contract, role-specific Job Description, Employee Handbook, Care in Ireland Handbook and New Starter Onboarding Checklist are required.

## International pathway
Candidates recorded as not living in Ireland additionally receive the International Recruitment & Visa Sponsorship Pack, Welcome to Ireland Pack and International Relocation & First-Month Checklist.

## Acceptance gates
An email being sent is not itself proof of completion. The portal should retain candidate identity, expiring packet access records, signature state, audit events, and permanent staff identity linkage when the candidate enters Selected, Offer Issued or Onboarding status.

Employment, immigration, tax and safeguarding decisions remain subject to applicable authority and BIMED approval.
