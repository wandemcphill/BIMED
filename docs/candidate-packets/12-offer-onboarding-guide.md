# BIMED Offer & Onboarding Guide

## Welcome
This guide explains what to expect after BIMED selects you for a role. Your contract, role-specific job description and applicable policies are issued separately for review and signature.

## Before your start date
- Review the employment contract and job description carefully.
- Complete all required signatures and return requested information promptly.
- Provide outstanding identity, qualification, reference and compliance evidence requested by BIMED.
- Confirm your agreed start date, location, working pattern and first-day instructions with the recruitment team.

## International candidates
Do not travel or begin employment based only on receipt of an onboarding email. Any required immigration, employment permit, entry clearance and related permissions must be completed or confirmed through the applicable process before lawful commencement.

## Your first day
Expect an induction covering your role, safeguarding, confidentiality, infection prevention, health and safety, incident reporting, communication and local workplace procedures. You will be introduced to your line manager and provided with the information needed for your assigned duties.

## Your first month
BIMED may use supervised practice, mandatory training, check-ins and competency reviews during onboarding. Raise questions early and report any safety or wellbeing concern through the channels provided during induction.

## Payroll and personal details
Provide payroll information only through the secure process supplied by BIMED. Never email passwords, PINs, card security codes or one-time authentication codes.

## Important
This guide is an operational orientation document, not a substitute for your contract, immigration decision, statutory requirement or BIMED policy. Applicable legal and regulatory requirements take precedence.
