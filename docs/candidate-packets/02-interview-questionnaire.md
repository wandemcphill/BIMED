# BIMED Healthcare Interview Questionnaire

**Document status:** Controlled template  
**Audience:** Applicants invited to interview  
**Owner:** BIMED Healthcare HR / Recruitment

## Candidate instructions

Answer each question honestly and using your own experience. Where a question asks for an example, explain the situation, what you did, why you acted that way, and the outcome. Do not include unnecessary confidential information about previous service users.

## Section A — Motivation and role understanding

1. Why do you want to work for BIMED Healthcare?
2. What does person-centred care mean to you in practice?
3. What do you understand to be the responsibilities of a support worker in relation to dignity, privacy, choice, and independence?
4. What does professional reliability mean when working shifts?

## Section B — Health and wellbeing observation

5. Describe a time you noticed something wrong with a service user's health or wellbeing, and what you did about it.
6. What changes in a service user's condition would make you escalate immediately?
7. Tell us about a time you had to remain calm when a service user became distressed, confused, or upset.

## Section C — Safeguarding and professional boundaries

8. What would you do if you suspected abuse, neglect, exploitation, or unsafe practice?
9. Describe a time you maintained confidentiality when handling sensitive information.
10. What would you do if a colleague asked you to do something outside your competence or training?

## Section D — Teamwork and communication

11. Give an example of a difficult conversation with a colleague, service user, or family member and how you handled it.
12. How would you respond if handover information appeared incomplete or inconsistent with what you observed?
13. Describe a time you worked as part of a multidisciplinary or care team.

## Section E — Practical care and safety

14. What steps do you take before assisting with a task that may carry a manual-handling or infection risk?
15. What would you do if a service user's care plan appeared unsafe, outdated, or inconsistent with their current presentation?

## Section F — International applicants

16. What is your current country of residence?
17. Do you currently have permission to live and work in Ireland?
18. Would you require an Irish employment permit or sponsorship support?
19. What is your expected relocation timeframe if appointed?
20. What practical arrangements have you considered for accommodation, travel, finances, and arrival in Ireland?

## Closing questions

21. What is one quality you believe makes an excellent healthcare support worker?
22. What training or area of professional development would you most like to strengthen?
23. Is there anything else you would like BIMED to know about your suitability for this role?

## Interviewer notes

Record objective evidence, not assumptions. Note examples of safe decision-making, escalation, safeguarding awareness, communication, empathy, reliability, and willingness to work within competence. Any candidate-specific decision should follow BIMED's approved recruitment criteria and scoring process.
