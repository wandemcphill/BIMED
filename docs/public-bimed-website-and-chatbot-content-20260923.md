# BIMED Public Website + Recruitment Chatbot Content Pack
## Drafted 23 September 2026

> **Purpose:** Public-facing content for BIMED Healthcare's main website and recruitment chatbot. Candidate-specific records, internal administration notes, private commercial accommodation terms, assessment questions, permit IDs, invoices and internal staff communications must never be exposed through the public chatbot.

## 1. BIMED public positioning

### Care that supports people and families
BIMED Healthcare provides person-centred care and support designed around individual needs, dignity, safety, wellbeing and meaningful everyday life.

As BIMED expands its care homes, facilities and community services, public enquiries can cover both:
- support for older people and families caring for elderly relatives; and
- support for children and adults with disabilities or additional needs, including respite and family support where appropriate.

Care can be delivered through BIMED care settings and, where the service is available and appropriate, through tailored support in the person's home or community.

### Existing public service themes
- Post-surgical assistance
- Physical disability support
- Dementia support
- Personal care
- Community and social support
- Support with independence, routines and meaningful activity

Final service availability, location and care package are subject to assessment, staffing, safeguarding requirements and current capacity.

## 4. Recruitment Centre

### Recruitment journey

1. Initial enquiry or CV through an official BIMED channel.
2. BIMED reviews the profile against genuine recruitment needs.
3. Selected candidates receive a private portal invitation.
4. Candidate completes the guided application.
5. BIMED conducts interviews and required checks.
6. Successful candidates receive the relevant employment/offer documentation.
7. International candidates complete the applicable accommodation, employment-permit and immigration workflow.
8. Onboarding and start arrangements are completed before employment begins.

The public website must clearly state that submitting an enquiry or CV does not automatically create a portal account, guarantee a vacancy or guarantee an employment offer.

## 5. Application integrity

BIMED has a zero-tolerance approach to false, fraudulent or materially misleading information in recruitment applications.

Candidates must provide accurate and verifiable information and documents throughout recruitment, employment-permit and immigration processes.

Where BIMED identifies information or documentation that it reasonably considers false, fraudulent, materially misleading or deliberately incomplete, the application may be cancelled and BIMED may provide relevant information to the appropriate authorities where applicable.

Candidates should understand that false or misleading information can have serious consequences for employment-permit and immigration matters.

Never submit altered, fabricated or misleading documents.

## 6. International employment-permit workflow

### Important BIMED process

Signing an employment contract does **not** by itself cause BIMED to immediately submit an employment-permit application.

Under BIMED's recruitment process, where BIMED-led accommodation is required, the applicable accommodation payment/arrangement must be completed first. Once the required accommodation payment and necessary information are received, BIMED aims to initiate the employment-permit process within **72 working hours**.

This **72-working-hour period is a BIMED internal processing target**, not a DETE processing deadline.

### After BIMED submits

Employment Permits Online requires employer, employee and agent users to have portal accounts. The employee may receive direct communication or prompts requiring action.

Candidates must:
- monitor the email address used for the permit process regularly;
- respond promptly to any DETE request;
- provide complete and accurate information;
- complete their own required steps in the government system; and
- tell BIMED promptly if they encounter a genuine technical or process issue.

BIMED cannot complete personal steps that DETE requires the employee to perform.

Official DETE information confirms that Employment Permits Online is used to apply, submit documents, make payments and track permit applications, and that employer, employee and agent users have portal accounts.

https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/

## 7. Accommodation verification and Irish address

For BIMED's international recruitment workflow, an **Irish residential/accommodation address is required by BIMED before the permit process is initiated**, and the address information supplied by the candidate must correspond with the accommodation information BIMED provides for the application.

This is a BIMED recruitment/verification requirement. The website should not present BIMED's internal requirement as a universal statement of Irish law without legal/DETE confirmation.

BIMED introduced its preferred BIMED-led accommodation pathway partly because accommodation information needs to be genuine, complete and capable of verification when required.

BIMED has encountered past cases involving accommodation documentation or arrangements that could not be satisfactorily verified. Examples supplied by BIMED management include:
- fraudulent or incomplete accommodation documents; and
- genuine private accommodation arrangements where the landlord would not respond to DETE or corporate verification requests.

The website should describe these as **BIMED's past recruitment experience**, not as a statement that every private accommodation arrangement will fail.

## 8. BIMED-led accommodation

### Public positioning

BIMED-led accommodation is a **temporary, subsidised accommodation arrangement** facilitated through private accommodation/building-management providers in Ireland.

BIMED does not represent itself as the owner of the buildings or as a permanent accommodation landlord.

BIMED has private accommodation arrangements with building-management providers in locations including **Cork, Galway and Dublin**.

**WEBGEEK TECHNOLOGIES is an accommodation partner of BIMED.**

Private lease terms, negotiated commercial rates, building-management correspondence and internal allocation arrangements are not public information.

### What happens after the initial arrangement?

When the BIMED-led arrangement ends, the recruit may, where available, enter into a new accommodation agreement directly with the building-management provider or arrange alternative accommodation elsewhere.

Any later private lease is between the recruit and the accommodation provider and does not involve BIMED.

## 10. Accommodation payment safety

BIMED does not receive staff-accommodation payments into the BIMED corporate account because staff accommodation is not a BIMED corporate accommodation service.

Candidates must use the **account name, IBAN and bank details shown on the official BIMED accommodation invoice**.

Candidates should send proof of payment to BIMED so the payment can be recorded and followed up.

A European SEPA account outside Ireland, including a German IBAN, does not by itself make an accommodation payment illegitimate. BIMED's accommodation arrangements are private arrangements with accommodation/building-management providers.

**Critical rule:** BIMED will not validate or accept responsibility for third-party payments that do not correspond with the applicant's name.

If a proposed payment is to be made by another person, or if bank details change, the applicant must contact BIMED through an official email address before paying.

The public chatbot must never invent or confirm bank details. It should direct candidates to the official invoice and the official BIMED email channels for verification.

## 11. Family-arranged accommodation

Applicants already admitted through the BIMED recruitment portal who have relatives in Ireland may request a special family-arranged accommodation route.

BIMED may permit this only where the proposed arrangement is independently verifiable and meets BIMED's requirements.

The proposed accommodation must be in **Cork, Galway or Dublin**.

The supporting family member/relative must be able to provide the evidence BIMED requires to verify the arrangement, including applicable proof of Irish address and evidence of their immigration/nationality status.

Under BIMED's current stated policy, the supporting person must not be relying solely on a student visa/permission or temporary work permission that does not satisfy BIMED's verification requirements.

The supporting person may also need to participate in the employment-permit process with their supporting address/status evidence where required.

**Important:** family support does not guarantee employment-permit or visa approval. The final decision remains with the relevant authority.

Applicants already admitted who wish to request family-arranged accommodation should email:
**manager@bimedhealthcare.com**

## 12. Shared accommodation

Accepted applicants can request shared accommodation.

If two accepted applicants already know each other, the portal can use the other accepted applicant's BIMED email/identifier to create the applicable shared arrangement, subject to eligibility and availability.

Shared accommodation:
- maximum two professionals;
- same-gender sharing only;
- applicants can request to be paired with another eligible applicant.

Applicants must not request or reject a roommate based on nationality, ethnicity or similar personal characteristics. BIMED should not disclose another candidate's private information for roommate selection.

The chatbot may discuss the rules above but must never reveal another candidate's identity, nationality, ethnicity, address, contact details, application status or other private information.

## 15. Public recruitment chatbot rules

The chatbot should answer only from an approved public knowledge base.

It should:
- explain public recruitment procedures;
- explain public accommodation rules;
- explain the difference between BIMED-led and family-arranged accommodation;
- explain official payment-safety rules;
- explain the applicant's responsibilities after a permit submission;
- explain available public services and care enquiries;
- provide official BIMED contact addresses;
- clearly distinguish BIMED policy from Irish government requirements;
- direct individual case-specific matters to BIMED staff.

It must never reveal:
- candidate records;
- interview answers or question pools;
- internal assessments;
- permit IDs;
- staff records;
- private accommodation addresses;
- invoices or bank details;
- internal notes;
- another applicant's details;
- private commercial lease terms;
- passwords, OTPs or security information.

### Safe fallback

> I can provide BIMED's published recruitment and accommodation information, but I cannot access or disclose individual candidate records. For a case-specific matter, please contact BIMED through one of the official addresses on this website.
