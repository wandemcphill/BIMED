As I finalise the recruitment documents and develop the proposed digital candidate application process, I would like to make sure that everything accurately reflects Bimed Healthcare's actual employment terms, recruitment procedures and policies.

Rather than making assumptions or inserting generic contractual provisions, I would appreciate it if you could provide the information below. Where you already have an existing policy, contract, template or document containing the information, you can simply attach it rather than retyping it.

Full legal company name

Authorised signatory details

Exact job titles being recruited

3. Employment terms
Salary/hourly rate

Any other employment conditions

4. Recruitment process
Please confirm the intended sequence from CV submission through shortlisting, interview, selection, offer, permit/visa processing and onboarding.

5. Interview and assessment
Please provide any existing interview questions, scoring criteria or assessment procedures currently used by Bimed.

6. Overseas recruitment and employment permits
Please confirm which roles Bimed intends to recruit internationally, whether Bimed sponsors eligible candidates, who manages the permit process, whether Bimed uses an immigration/employment-permit adviser and which costs are covered by Bimed versus the candidate.

7. Employment contracts and offer documentation
Please provide any existing employment contract, offer letter, employment confirmation or HR template currently used by Bimed.

8. Supporting documents and checks
Please confirm the documents required from Ireland-based and overseas candidates, including references, Garda vetting/background checks, police clearance, qualifications, passport, driving licence and any other requirements.

9. Relocation/reimbursement
I understand that Bimed does not provide a direct relocation package but may reimburse eligible expenses after the main applicant completes the three-month probationary period, potentially covering a family of up to three people.

Before this is included in official candidate documentation, please confirm the exact policy, including eligible expenses, maximum amounts, definition of "family of three", evidence/receipts, approval process and any conditions attached.

10. GDPR and privacy
Please provide Bimed's privacy notice, recruitment privacy statement, data-retention requirements and any existing GDPR documentation relevant to recruitment.

11. Care in Ireland / Life in Ireland handbooks
Please provide any company-specific information you would like included, particularly arrival arrangements, induction, accommodation guidance, transport, payroll, banking, training, uniform, emergency contacts and support provided to international employees.

12. Digital recruitment portal
Please confirm the recruitment personnel who should receive application notifications and eventually have access to the recruitment dashboard.

The proposed system will remain invitation-only. Candidates selected by Bimed will receive a private link by email and complete the application online. Supporting documents will not be uploaded to the portal. Candidates will instead be instructed to send their documents separately to the appropriate Bimed recruitment email address.

The objective is to make the recruitment process faster and easier for both candidates and the recruitment team while maintaining a professional and consistent candidate experience.

I will continue developing the system while awaiting this information. Any areas requiring Bimed-specific confirmation will remain configurable until the relevant information is provided.

Thank you, and please feel free to send the information in stages if that is easier. Existing documents can simply be attached.
